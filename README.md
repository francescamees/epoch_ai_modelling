# Epoch AI Modelling

Artificial intelligence (AI) is expanding rapidly, with generative models such as ChatGPT in- tensifying concerns about electricity usage and environmental impact, as training energy demand is doubling annually. For companies involved in AI development these pressures create both sustainabil- ity and financial challenges, making early and reliable estimates of training power draw critical for budgeting, profitability, and compliance with carbon reporting. This paper aims to build a machine learning model to estimate the power draw required to train AI models using a cross-sectional dataset from Epoch AI. To address heavy missingness, extreme skewness, and sparsity, targeted pre-processing was employed, including median and mode imputation, log-transformations, one-hot encoding, scaling, outlier removal, and grouping of multi-categorical variables. Eleven distinct data versions were then constructed by applying different combinations of these pre-processing steps. Seven ML models were evaluated on each data version with 5-fold Cross Validation and Leave One Out Cross Validation, using RMSE and MAE as primary performance metrics and R2 as a complementary statistic. To achieve the strongest performing model, Random Forest and Elastic Net were ensembled to exploit the strategy of tree models while stabilizing linear relationships. Findings emphasize trade-offs of ensemble accuracy versus computational and retraining costs, while generalizability is constrained by subjective grouping criteria, imputed values, and rapid shifts in AI technology.
